About

Easemotion is a motion design and illustration studio based in Hong Kong.

With years of experience spanning broadcast design, channel branding, event visuals, product visualization, and creative illustration, we bring a refined aesthetic to every project — from TV channel rebrands and LED screen content to projection mapping and brand identity work.

Our work has been featured across major networks and platforms including Fox, KIX, CM+, NowTV, HKEX, and numerous brands and cultural institutions in Hong Kong and beyond.

We believe in motion that moves — visuals that tell a story, capture attention, and leave a lasting impression.
